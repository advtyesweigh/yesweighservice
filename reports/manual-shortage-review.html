What a future sale will do

  • Zoho stock goes down: audited quantity goes down by the same amount. The +/− stays.
  • Zoho stock goes up with no pending goods receipt: the +/− stays. Audited quantity moves with Zoho.
  • A goods receipt that is not in Zoho yet: the difference includes that inbound until Zoho posts it, then the previous difference remains.
  • A new shelf count replaces the difference. That is the only way a shortage closes.
